# Keplix AI

Summarize. Learn. Understand.

## Vision
Build an AI-powered content understanding platform, not just a YouTube summarizer.

## Core Pillars
- **Mobile-first**
- **Dark & Light mode support**
- **Clean, Apple/Notion-inspired UI**
- **Fast and SEO-first**

## Supported Content Types

### Initial Tools
- Link
- Audio
- Text

### Future Roadmap
- YouTube
- X (Twitter)
- Instagram
- PDFs
- Articles
- Transcripts

## Development Rules & Conventions
- **Modification Policy:** Never rewrite existing files unless explicitly asked.
- **Planning:** Always explain your plan before making major changes.
- **Architecture:** Keep components modular and reusable.
- **Best Practices:** Follow Astro and Tailwind CSS idiomatic patterns.
